# Giving feedback on Say AI

Send feedback directly to Anton in the conversation where he invited you to test.
A short message or voice note is enough. There is no form to fill in; you can send
observations as you go or one message after the session.

## Tell us about usefulness

Describe what you wanted the bot to do and whether it helped. The most useful
feedback explains a real situation: who would ask these questions, how you handle
them today, and whether you would use this bot again. If you would not, explain
what prevents it. It is fine if the idea does not fit your needs.

Mention the confusing or unnecessary steps, where you needed help, and anything
that worked particularly well. Setup difficulty, connecting an API key, costs,
answer quality, and waiting time all matter. There is no need to give a numerical
score or suggest a technical solution.

For example:

> I answer delivery questions several times a week. The bot handled the simple
> ones, but I needed help connecting the API key. I would try it again if setup
> were easier.

## When something goes wrong

Include enough context for Anton to understand or reproduce the problem:

- What you tried to do and in which bot or manager screen.
- What you expected and what actually happened.
- The approximate time, with your time zone if it differs from Anton's.
- For an incorrect answer, the exact question and reply, plus the relevant
  sentence from your document if you are comfortable sharing it.
- For a button, form, or display problem, your device and Telegram client.

You do not need every detail to report a problem. A screenshot is optional;
remove personal information first. Never include API keys or bot tokens.

For example:

> Around 16:20 Berlin time, I asked my shop bot, "Can I return a sale item?"
> It answered that sale items cannot be returned. My document says they can be
> returned within 14 days. I expected that policy in the answer.

If you got stuck before creating a bot, tell Anton the last step you reached.
If the service is unavailable, send one message and wait for him to confirm it is
running again. Do not spend the session repeatedly retrying the same action.

Share only the material needed to explain the issue. If an example contains
confidential information, describe the problem first and agree with Anton on what
can be shared. Honest observations are more useful than a polished report.
